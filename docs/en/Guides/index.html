General Tips

Recommended Models: Mistral 7B or Llama 3 8B in Q4_K_M format offer a good balance between quality and speed.

Memory: 7B models require ~6GB of RAM. 13B+ models may require 16GB or more.

GPU: If you have a CUDA-compatible GPU, enable n_gpu_layers to significantly accelerate inference.
